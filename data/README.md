# Session 1.1 public-network data

The files in `data/public/` are committed, app-ready CSV copies of five **publicly documented** network datasets. They are generated by `scripts/build_session1_1_public_data.py`; do not edit their topology by hand.

| Files | Public source | Exact analytic scope |
|---|---|---|
| `florentine_business_*` | Statnet `ergm::flobusiness` | Undirected binary business-tie encoding for 16 Florentine families; verify 15 edges. |
| `sampson_liking_wave3_*` | Statnet `ergm::samplk3` | Directed binary liking nominations for the single third survey wave; verify 18 vertices and 56 ties. |
| `lazega_advice_*` | Oxford RSiena `LazegaLawyers.zip`, `ELadv.dat` and `ELattr.dat` | Directed binary advice relation for 71 lawyers; verify 892 ties. The arc records the respondent's named adviser. |
| `davis_affiliation_*` | Davis, Gardner, and Gardner (1941), as distributed in NetworkX/Statnet | Undirected binary woman–event incidence graph; verify 18 women, 14 events, and 89 ties. |
| `kapferer_sociational_*` | Statnet `ergm::kapferer` | Undirected binary first-period sociational relation for a selected 39-worker panel; verify 158 ties. |

## Important methodological restrictions

- The application fits only **binary static** ERGMs in Session 1.1.
- It preserves directionality and bipartite support; within-mode cells in the Davis network are structural zeros.
- It does not merge network layers. In particular, Lazega coworker/friendship relations are not inserted as ordinary predictors of advice.
- It does not treat `flobusiness` as an observed directed financial-flow graph, pooled Sampson nominations as one static network, or Kapferer interaction as a valued edge count.
- Every source-specific caveat is displayed in the app and retained in `dataset_catalog.json`.
